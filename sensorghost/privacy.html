Sensor Ghost Privacy Policy

Effective: October 3, 2026 · Applies to DockLight Sensor Ghost (com.docklightlabs.sensorghost)

Sensor Ghost is a read-only Android diagnostic overlay developed by DockLight Labs. It displays the selected device reading over other applications. It operates locally on the device, with no account or internet access required.

Data collection and sharing

Sensor Ghost does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, device readings, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or other user data with DockLight Labs or third parties. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not include an analytics or advertising SDK.

Readings processed on the device

When you select a source, the app reads Android's current screen-off timeout setting, charging and battery status, or one of the physical sensors made available to it by Android. Selected readings are used only to update the floating display. Sensor Ghost does not record a measurement history, upload these readings, or access the contents of apps appearing beneath its overlay. Sensor-specific access that Android does not permit is reported as unavailable.

Information stored locally

To remember your settings between launches, the app stores your selected display source, overlay visibility, floating-window position, and whether the overlay was enabled. These preferences are held in Android's app-private storage on your device and are not transmitted. You can clear them using Android Settings > Apps > DockLight Sensor Ghost > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions and foreground service

Display over other apps: You enable this Android special access to allow the floating readout to appear above other apps. You can revoke it in Android settings.

Foreground service: The user-started monitoring service keeps the readout current while the overlay is running and provides a persistent notification. You can stop the overlay from within Sensor Ghost or its notification. The service is not used to send data off-device.

Accounts, payments and children

The app requires no registration or account. If purchased on Google Play, purchase and payment handling occur through Google under its own policies; Sensor Ghost does not receive or store payment card details. Sensor Ghost is a general-purpose device utility and is not directed specifically at children.

Requests, changes and contact

DockLight Labs retains no off-device app data to delete or provide. For privacy questions, contact docklightlabs@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated text and effective date will appear on this page.
