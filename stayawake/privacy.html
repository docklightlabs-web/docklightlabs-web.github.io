DockLight StayAwake Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Applies to DockLight StayAwake (com.docklightlabs.stayawake)

DockLight StayAwake is an offline Android utility developed by DockLight Labs. It uses device charging status and physical orientation to apply the screen timeout behavior you enable. It operates entirely on your device.

Data collection and sharing

StayAwake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, sensor or charging readings, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or other user data with DockLight Labs or third parties. It requests no INTERNET permission and includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, accounts, or subscriptions.

Device information processed locally

StayAwake checks charging state and physical orientation to decide when to apply or restore your chosen Android screen timeout. These readings are processed only on your device. The app does not create a history of measurements or access content in other apps.

Local storage

The enabled preference, original screen timeout, and temporary override state are saved locally in Android app-private preferences so the utility can restore your prior settings. DockLight Labs does not receive this information. The app data is removed when you clear its storage or uninstall it.

Permissions and user control

Modify system settings: You explicitly grant Android's permission so StayAwake can adjust your device's screen timeout. You can revoke that permission in Android settings.

Foreground service: An ongoing notification accompanies the enabled local charging/orientation monitor so it can apply and restore screen timeout behavior while other apps are open. You can disable monitoring inside StayAwake.

Start after reboot: If previously enabled, the utility resumes its chosen behavior after the device restarts.

Before uninstalling, disable StayAwake within the app to restore your previous timeout. If you remove the app while active, choose your preferred timeout manually in Android Settings.

Accounts, payments, and children

No account is required. Any Google Play app purchase is processed by Google under its policies; DockLight Labs does not receive or store payment card information. The app is a general-purpose device utility, not directed specifically to children.

Contact and changes

DockLight Labs holds no off-device app data to retrieve or delete. Questions may be sent to docklightlabs@gmail.com. Any changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
