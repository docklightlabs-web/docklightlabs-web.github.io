Straight answers

Before you reserve

What if it never ships? Am I paying for unfinished software?

No. There is no prelaunch checkout, deposit or installment contract. We only collect expressions of purchase interest. If no product ships, nothing is charged. If it does ship, you review the finished offer and decide whether to buy then.

What if I stop paying before I own it?

An installment license is not fully owned or transferable before the last payment clears. The exact missed-payment, cancellation, grace-period and customer-data-access terms will be published in the written agreement before checkout; we are not asking you to accept unspecified terms or pay anything now.

What happens after all 26 installments?

Installment billing ends and the latest version available on the date your final payment clears becomes yours to keep. Updates and support are included throughout the scheduled 26-month term.

Can I transfer a license?

Not while payments remain outstanding. Transfer eligibility begins at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the calendar day after the final payment clears. A $25 transfer-administration fee applies.

Are there payment-processing charges?

Any applicable payment-method fees would be disclosed before actual checkout and charged only where permitted. No payment method or charge is involved in this reservation.

What happens when support/update coverage ends?

The last version you were entitled to remains usable. Ongoing updates and support are optional, not mandatory rent. Post-entitlement pricing will be published before paid checkout opens.
